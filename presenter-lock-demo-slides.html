Presenter Lock

Control slides with hand gestures.
No remote. No mouse. No touching the laptop.

Team of 4 • Working model, live demo

1. The Problem

  • A teacher or presenter has to keep walking back to the laptop to change slides.
  • Or they lose the remote.
  • That breaks the flow of the presentation.

2. Gap in Old Gesture Demos

  • False trigger: they listen always, so slides change by mistake when you talk with your hands.
  • Hijack: any hand in front of the camera can change slides.
  • No pointer: you still need a mouse to point at a diagram.
  • No feedback: you do not know if your gesture worked.

3. Our Solution - Presenter Lock

  • Palm 1 second = Unlock. After 5 seconds idle, it auto-locks again.
  • First hand only. A second hand is ignored - no hijack.
  • Finger pointer. Index finger = red laser pointer on screen.
  • Feedback. Green flash on accept, red on ignore.
  • Fist = Lock immediately.

LIVE DEMO - Try it now

Judge waves a hand - nothing happens, it is locked.
Presenter shows palm 1 second - green, unlocked.
Swipe right / left - slide changes. Index finger - pointer moves.

4. How It Works & Future

  • Laptop webcam only - no extra hardware.
  • Python + MediaPipe hand tracking + OpenCV + PyAutoGUI.
  • Works for classrooms, and in future for hospitals and labs where touching is not allowed.
